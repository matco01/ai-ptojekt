Principles

It sells so that people come back.

i.

Never pushy

If a cheaper option suits the customer better, it says so. If they'd rather browse, it lets them – and only speaks up when they're truly stuck.

ii.

Never makes things up

Prices, specs, deals and delivery come only from your data. When it doesn't know, it says so and points to your contact.

iii.

Your data stays yours

It records behaviour only with the visitor's consent, masks e-mails and numbers in questions and deletes old data by itself. Each shop sees only its own.

Questions

What people ask us.

What is Sella?

Sella is an AI salesperson for online shops – a chatbot that behaves like a good shop assistant. It greets customers when they arrive, asks if they'd like help, recommends products from your catalogue, compares them and adds them to the cart. It chats and speaks, in Czech and English.

Which online shops does Sella work with?

Shoptet, WordPress with WooCommerce, Shopify, Upgates and any custom website – you add one line of code, directly or through Google Tag Manager. A video guide shows every system step by step, and if you'd rather not, we'll add it for you.

How much does Sella cost?

Plans start at CZK 990 a month (final price – we are not VAT registered) and differ only in AI messages and voice. You pay monthly by card, the invoice arrives by e-mail, and you can change or cancel any time. When the messages run out, the assistant switches off until the month ends – you never pay more.

Where does it get my products from?

From the XML feed you already have for Heureka, Zboží.cz or Google Merchant. Without a feed, it reads your products and page texts straight from your website – again every night, so it knows current prices and stock.

Won't it make things up?

It recommends only products from your catalogue, with prices and stock from your feed. Questions about delivery, payment and returns are answered only from the information you give it and the documents you upload. It doesn't invent discounts or specifications.

Is Sella GDPR compliant?

Yes. Visitor behaviour and questions are stored only with consent – from the shop's cookie banner, or when the customer writes to the assistant. E-mails and phone numbers in questions are masked, data is deleted after 90 days, and every shop gets a data processing agreement.

What will I see in my dashboard?

How many visitors used the assistant, how many of them bought compared with the rest, the average order, what customers ask, what they search for and don't find, and which products they view but don't buy. Plus AI advice on what to focus on.

How quickly can I get it running?

In minutes. Sign up, pay for a plan, add the code to your website and Sella reads the site by itself. Name, look, tone and shop information are set in your dashboard – it takes your colours and logo from your website on its own.
